Crisis Support

If you or someone you know is struggling, reach out. You are not alone.

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline — Call or text 988
  • Crisis Text Line — Text HOME to 741741
  • CPHS Counseling — Visit the front office

Study Techniques

Proven methods to study smarter, not harder.

  • Pomodoro Technique — 25 min focus, 5 min break. Use our Focus Mode!
  • Active Recall — Test yourself instead of re-reading notes
  • Spaced Repetition — Review material at increasing intervals
  • Cornell Notes — Divide your page into cues, notes, and summary

Physical Wellness

Move your body to boost your brain. Even small habits make a big difference.

  • Aim for 30 minutes of movement daily
  • Drink half your body weight in ounces of water
  • Get 8-10 hours of sleep (yes, really)
  • Stretch between classes to reset your focus

Mental Health Tips

Your mental health is just as important as your GPA.

  • Practice deep breathing when stressed (4-7-8 technique)
  • Take breaks — burnout is real
  • Talk to someone you trust when things feel heavy
  • Journaling for 5 minutes can reduce anxiety

Digital Wellness

Your phone is a tool, not a leash. Take control of your screen time.

  • Set screen time limits on social media apps
  • No screens 30 minutes before bed
  • Use Focus Mode during homework (it's right here!)
  • Turn off non-essential notifications

Nutrition Basics

What you eat affects how you think, feel, and perform in school.

  • Never skip breakfast — your brain needs fuel
  • Pack healthy snacks (nuts, fruit, granola bars)
  • Limit energy drinks — water is the real MVP
  • Eat the rainbow — variety = more nutrients
